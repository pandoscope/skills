# The waiver is a visible claim

**Rule (skills#46, check 4, ruling C7):** the per-ticket escape is
`no-update: <owner/repo#n> <why>` — logged as a claim, never
verified. Declining to update is thereby a visible act: the claim
lands in the compliance log, where a pattern of waivers is evidence.

## What the fixture freezes

The same declared ticket as kata 55, with the waiver line written and
a reason given. Nothing else changes.

## Expected

The turn seals; the waiver rides the verdict detail as a claim.
