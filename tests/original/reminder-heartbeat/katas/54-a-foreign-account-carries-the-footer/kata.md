# A foreign account carries the footer

**Rule (skills#46, check 14, account safeguard):** with distinct
principal and agent accounts configured via `AGENT_ACCOUNTS`,
authorship beats the footer as the human/agent discriminator — and a
footer on a foreign account (or an agent account posting bare) means
the attribution contract itself is broken. By ruling, that fails
loudly; opt-in by construction, since a session without the variable
keeps the footer-only reading.

## What the fixture freezes

A fetched comment whose body carries the footer while its author is
`the-principal` — not in `AGENT_ACCOUNTS` — under a summary declaring
`reviews: none`.

## Expected

The turn blocks on the contract anomaly, not on the persistence
question: every footer-based reading is suspect until the account or
the footer habit is fixed.
