## Grilling Session

A [session](session.md) of [grilling](grilling.md): relentless
one-question-at-a-time interview of user about plan/design, walking every
branch of decision tree until shared understanding (`derived/grilling`). Facts: look up in codebase. Decisions: put to user, with recommended answer. Required before load-bearing artifacts — tickets, specs, ADRs, complex decisions — unless user explicitly skips.
