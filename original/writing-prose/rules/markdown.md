# Markdown rules

These rules hold for Markdown files in a repository, skill files and files loaded into every session.

- `sembr` [H] Break new or rewritten prose at sentence or clause boundaries, one per line.
- `reflow` [F] Never reflow untouched lines, and put a deliberate mass reflow in its own style commit listed in the blame-ignore file.
- `line-ref` [H] Refer to a passage by quoting its anchor text, not by its line number.
- `glossary-marking` [H] Link the first mention of a glossary term, bold every later mention that means the term, and leave the everyday word unmarked.
- `long-line` [H] Split a sentence too long to break at a clause, or keep it on one line, and never break it at a fixed width.
