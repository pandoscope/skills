## Premature Completion

*Failure mode.*
Ending a step before it is genuinely done, because attention slipped to being done.
A tug-of-war between visible [post-completion steps](post-completion-steps.md)
and how sharply the [completion criterion](completion-criterion.md) can be checked.
Raise the criterion's rung first — local and cheap.
Hide later steps only when it stays interpretive *and* the rush is observed,
and only across a real context boundary.

_Avoid_: premature closure, rushing, shortcutting
