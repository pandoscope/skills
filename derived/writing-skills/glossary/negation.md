## Negation

*Failure mode.*
Steering by prohibition, which drags the forbidden behavior into context and makes it more available.
*Do not think of an elephant* names the elephant.
Cure: state the target behavior, so the banned one is never spoken.
A prohibition earns its place only as a guardrail that cannot be phrased positively,
and even then pairs with the positive target.
