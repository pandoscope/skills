## Enforcement Boundary

The line between what a hook or test checks and what the skill says in prose.
Anything mechanically checkable belongs on the hook side:
an enforced rule costs zero tokens at runtime and cannot be skimmed past,
where a well-worded one costs [context load](context-load.md) every run and still relies on attention.
Prose keeps what needs judgment.

_Avoid_: automation, guardrail, validation
