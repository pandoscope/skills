## Duplication

*Failure mode.*
One meaning stated in more than one place.
Costs maintenance, costs tokens, and inflates the meaning's apparent rank —
repetition reads as emphasis.
The accidental inverse of a [leading word](leading-word.md),
which repeats a token on purpose and never the meaning.
Cure: one home, referenced from anywhere else that needs it.

_Avoid_: repetition, redundancy
