## Duplication

*Failure mode.*
One meaning given more than one [single source of truth](single-source-of-truth.md).
Costs maintenance and tokens, and inflates the meaning's rank on the [information hierarchy](information-hierarchy.md).
The accidental inverse of a [leading word](leading-word.md),
which repeats a token on purpose and never the meaning.

_Avoid_: repetition, redundancy
