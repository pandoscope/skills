## Derivation Pin

The exact upstream commit a derived skill was taken from, recorded in its own metadata.
Without it, "derived from" names a moving target and the [verbatim baseline](verbatim-baseline.md) cannot be reconstructed.
Updating means diffing the pin against upstream's current state,
folding the changes into the derivation, and bumping the pin.

_Avoid_: version, source link, provenance
