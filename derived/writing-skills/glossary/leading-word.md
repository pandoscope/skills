## Leading Word

A compact concept already in the model's pretraining that the agent thinks with
while running the skill — *tracer bullet*, *fog of war*, *red*, *blast radius*.
Repeated as a token, it accumulates a distributed definition
and anchors a region of behavior in the fewest tokens.
Coined words recruit no priors: you pay in definition tokens what a pretrained word gives free.

_Avoid_: keyword, term, motif
