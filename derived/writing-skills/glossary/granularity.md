## Granularity

How finely skills are divided.
Every cut spends one of the two loads —
more [model-invoked](model-invoked.md) skills spend [context load](context-load.md),
more [user-invoked](user-invoked.md) skills spend [cognitive load](cognitive-load.md).
Two cuts earn it: by invocation (a distinct [leading word](leading-word.md) should trigger it, or another skill must reach it)
and by sequence (hiding [post-completion steps](post-completion-steps.md)).

_Avoid_: chunking, modularity
